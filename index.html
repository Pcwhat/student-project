Project Background

Why We Built This Showcase Website

Addressing common difficulties faced by engineering students and faculty during semester project reviews.

Avoid Duplicate Project Ideas

Junior students often wonder what projects seniors have already built. This shared portal allows students to explore previous work so they can build improved, innovative projects instead of repeating old topics.

Clear Team Member Attribution

In group assignments, it is sometimes hard to see who worked on frontend, logic, or hardware. Our case study page lists each member with their college ERP ID and exact module responsibility.

Fast Viva Examination Review

During end-semester lab exams, faculty and external examiners can immediately view project abstracts, tech stacks, live links, and grading rubrics in two minutes without flipping through heavy paper files.
